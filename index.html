🫧 How to Play Iridescent Bubble Sanctuary
🌬️
Airflow Currents: Gently move your cursor or drag your finger near the bubble. The air velocity vector pushes and elastically deforms the bubble.
💨
Fan Puff: Click anywhere, tap the Puff button, or hit Spacebar to blast an upward shockwave puff that lofts the bubble aloft!
⚠️
Perilous Hazards: Watch out for glinting sewing needles, sneaky furry cat claws creeping in, and spinning ceiling fan blades drifting through!
🎻
Adaptive Orchestral Pizzicato: Web Audio plucks strings peacefully in safety, but accelerates frantically into high-tension chamber arpeggios when hazards get close!
🌿
Zen Sanctuary: Prefer pure chill relaxation? Toggle to Zen Mode (Z) so the bubble squishes and bouncily deflects off sharp objects without popping!
👶
Baby Bubbles (B): Spawn mini orbiting baby bubbles that flutter around the mother bubble and shield it from stray hazards!
🪩
Disco Soap Mode (D): Turn on hyper-rainbow iridescent specular lighting and upbeat groove pizzicato!
